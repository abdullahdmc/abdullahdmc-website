Dr. Md. Abdullah Saeed Khan

Epidemiologist and public health expert, Assistant Professor of Epidemiology (MoHFW, Bangladesh), and DPhil candidate in Clinical Medicine at the University of Oxford.

AKYour portrait here

Dr. Khan works in the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare as an Assistant Professor of Epidemiology. He is a research consultant with Pi Research Development Center and an advisor to the Public Health Promotion and Development Society.

His studies cover infectious diseases, noncommunicable diseases, quality of life, economic evaluation and clinical trials. His current work develops machine-learning prediction algorithms for febrile illness management, with the South East Asian Community Trial Network (SEACTN) at the Mahidol-Oxford Tropical Medicine Research Unit (MORU).

He has more than 100 peer-reviewed publications and over 2,000 citations on Google Scholar (end of 2025).
