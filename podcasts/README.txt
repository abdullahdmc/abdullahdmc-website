PODCAST ARCHIVE (on this computer only)
=======================================

Episodes are published on Spotify (Spotify for Creators). This folder is only your own archive:
nothing here is uploaded to the website.

HOW AN EPISODE REACHES THE WEBSITE
----------------------------------
1. Make the episode in Gemini Notebook (Audio Overview) from your AI in Health Research roundups.
2. Upload it to your show on creators.spotify.com and publish it there.
3. That's all. The daily website task (around 11:08, or when you next open the Claude app)
   sees the new episode in your Spotify feed and creates the podcast post on abdullahdmc.com
   with a player, a "Listen on Spotify" button and links to the roundups it covers.

KEEPING A COPY HERE (optional)
------------------------------
Save your downloaded audio in "inbox" or "published", whichever you like. A name such as

    YYYY-MM-DD Episode title.mp3      e.g.  2026-10-29 AI integration updates.m4a

keeps the files in date order.
